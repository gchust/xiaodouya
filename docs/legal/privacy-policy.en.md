# Xiaodouya (小豆芽) Privacy Policy

- **Effective date:** `[Effective date]`
- **Operator:** `[Operator name]`, `[Operator address]`
- **Contact:** `[Contact email]`

Xiaodouya is an app for a family to record a baby's growth together. This policy explains what information we handle, why, who can see it, where it is stored, how long it is kept and what you can do about it.

Two markers are used only while this is a draft and must be dealt with before publishing (see section 17):

- `〔In development〕`: the feature is still being built, and the text describes the intended behaviour.
- `〔To do〕`: something the operator still has to put in place.

## 1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Xiaodouya service operated by `[Operator name]`, meaning the app and our servers.

We do not control the data in these cases, and this policy does not cover that data:

- **You changed the "server address" on the sign-in screen to another server.** Your data goes to that server, and its operator is responsible for it.
- **Your family's admin set up their own storage under Family → Storage.** Photos, videos, recordings and document files go to the provider the admin chose. Other records stay in our database.
- **Content you choose to share yourselves.** For example, a growth book PDF you export and send to someone.

## 2. You provide information on your child's behalf

Xiaodouya is for adults. Children are not our users. Most of what you enter is about your child, so please use the app only if you are the child's parent or guardian, or have their permission. See section 13.

**What we treat as sensitive:** your child's health records, birth information, documents and document files. We use them only to provide the app's features to your family. We do not use them for advertising or to train models, and we do not sell them.

**Hidden information in photos:** photos and files can contain embedded details such as the time, place and device. The app does not read or use these to locate you, but it also does not currently remove them from the original file, so they may be stored with it. If this concerns you, please remove them before uploading.

### What we don't do

- The app does not use location services, read your contacts or calendar, or connect to HealthKit or Health Connect.
- The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- Fingerprint and face recognition are handled by your phone's system. We only learn "succeeded" or "failed" and never receive biometric data.
- We do not sell your information.

## 11. Security

What we do:

- Traffic between the app, the server and storage uses HTTPS.
- We keep only an scrypt hash of passwords. Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes on the server, and in the system's secure storage on the phone.
- Files are accessed through short-lived signed links: 10 minutes for documents, 1 hour for other files. While a link is valid, anyone who has it can open the file.
- Database access is limited to the server, and permissions are checked on the server.
- Sign-in and invitation attempts are limited.
- Our server code is designed not to write request contents, document numbers, tokens or signed links to logs.
- Backups are encrypted before upload.

Limits you should know about:

- **Document number masking happens only on screen.** The app shows something like `S12•••••A` by default, and one tap reveals the whole number. Document numbers are stored in plain text in the record contents in the database, like other records, with **no field-level encryption**.
- **This is not end-to-end encryption.** Supabase and Cloudflare state that they encrypt stored data (AES-256) with keys they manage. That protects against loss of physical media such as disks, but not against someone with access to the database or storage reading the contents. The operator is technically able to read your data. `〔To do〕` We access it only to investigate faults, handle your requests or meet legal requirements, and we log that access.
- **The database on your phone has no separate encryption.** It relies on your phone's own protection, so please set a screen lock.
- **The document lock is an on-screen safeguard.** It is off by default and checks you only when you open the document screen. If your phone has no screen lock, it cannot lock, and the app tells you so.
- We do not offer two-factor authentication. `〔In development〕` Email verification and self-service password reset are still being built. Today, if you forget your password, you have to contact the operator.
- The service has not had an independent third-party security audit.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and regulators as the applicable law requires. `〔To do: response procedure〕`
